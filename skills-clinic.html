THE G MTB METHOD

SCIENCE-BACKED TRAIL MASTERY

We don’t just tell you to "lean back" or "ride faster." We deconstruct modern mountain bike physics, bio-mechanics, and spatial perception into digestible drills that build lasting muscle memory.

Bike-Body Separation

Master lateral lean angles, hip hinge mobility, and range of movement so your bike moves freely beneath you without unsettling your center of gravity.

Slow-Mo Video Telemetry

Watch high-frame-rate video playback of your riding immediately trailside. Isolate micro-movements, eye-scan habits, and braking points in real time.

Cockpit & Sag Tuning

We start every clinic dialing in your suspension rebound, compression, tire pressures, brake lever reach, and cockpit ergonomics for maximum traction and zero arm pump.

Alpine Specific Skills

Prepare for Mustang & Everest terrain: loose high-altitude scree drift, tight alpine switchbacks, steep rock gardens, and high-altitude breathing cadences.

CLINIC CURRICULUM

CHOOSE YOUR PROGRESSION

From foundation fundamentals to high-altitude downhill drops, every module is structured to deliver noticeable breakthroughs in a single session.

MODULE 01 1 Day • Foundation

FOUNDATION & BALANCE

For beginners & trail riders looking to unlearn bad habits and build effortless control.

Core Syllabus Covered:

  • Neutral vs. Ready Position: Dynamic base of support over bottom bracket.
  • 1-Finger Braking Modulation: Preventing wheel lock-up & modulating front/rear bias.
  • Vision & Line Choice: Looking through turns rather than fixating on obstacles.
  • Steep Climbs & Safe Dismounts: Traction management on loose technical pinches.

MODULE 02 • MOST POPULAR 1-2 Days • Flow

FLOW & CORNERING MASTERY

For intermediate riders craving speed, cornering grip, and fluidity through rough sections.

Core Syllabus Covered:

  • High-Speed Berm Pumping: Generating exit speed through berm compression.
  • Flat Turn Traction & Counter-Steer: Foot pressure, outside pedal weighting & hip angulation.
  • Wheel Lifts & Bunny Hops: Unweighting over logs, roots, and trail ditches.
  • Braking Point Optimization: Scrubbing speed before the turn, not in it.
Terrain
Nagarkot & Hattiban Flow

MODULE 03 2-3 Days • Technical

HIGH-ALTITUDE TECHNICAL & DROPS

For enduro shredders preparing for big Himalayan expeditions or multi-stage races.

Core Syllabus Covered:

  • Steep Scree Switchbacks: Rear brake drift, nose pivots & pivot maneuvers.
  • Drop Progression: Roll-downs, flat drops, step-downs, and matching landing angles.
  • Rock Garden Commitment: Maintaining line momentum through chaotic boulder fields.
  • High-Altitude Pacing & Arm Pump: Tactical energy conservation over 1,500m descents.

KATHMANDU VALLEY TRAIL HUBS

Beginner & Flow

Hattiban Pine Forest

Pine needle loam, progressive switchbacks, natural pump rollers, and controlled root sections ideal for Level 1 & Level 2 foundation work.
