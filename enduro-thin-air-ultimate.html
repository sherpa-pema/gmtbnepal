COMPLETE TRIP ITINERARY

Upon arrival in Nepal, you’ll be greeted and transferred to Thamel, Kathmandu, where you’ll settle in, assemble your bikes with assistance from our master mechanic, and join a welcome dinner and comprehensive trip briefing to prepare for the adventure ahead.

A short flight from Kathmandu brings you to Pokhara, where you will head directly to the trails for the exciting and flowy Stupa DH ride, followed by an all-mountain ride around the lake. Enjoy a relaxing massage and explore the lakeside city while preparing for the Mustang adventure.

A scenic five-hour drive from Pokhara to Kalopani will be followed by a three-hour acclimatization ride through pine forests and around TiTi Lake, surrounded by the magnificent Dhaulagiri and Annapurna ranges. The day concludes in Lete, ready for the adventures ahead.

Ride from Kalopani to Marpha along the Kali Gandaki River through pine forests and Tukche, followed by the steep and technical Black Yak and scenic Yarsa trails. After the ride, transfer to Kagbeni for an overnight stay.

Begin with the warm-up trails of Perro Loco and Lubracation before tackling the spectacular Signature Lubra Trail, featuring technical singletrack and extraordinary Mustang scenery. After returning to Thini Village, a shuttle to the apple farm sets up an exhilarating final descent before an evening of rest in Jomsom.

Ascend to 4,200m, one of the highest points of the journey, before descending toward Muktinath on a rocky yet flowing trail with breathtaking mountain views. Continue with the Chasing Capra trail into Upper Mustang before driving to Ghilling for the night.

Discover some of Ghami’s finest trails, combined with a visit to one of Mustang’s oldest monasteries, before continuing through the spectacular trails toward Lo Manthang. Enjoy an unforgettable free-ride experience before relaxing and exploring Lo Manthang in the afternoon.

Experience one of the highlights of the tour with an epic ride combining D’Epic, Kali Canyon, and the smooth Coffee Break downhill. After completing the day’s trails, drive back to Jomsom for an evening of relaxation.

Ride the exciting Smooth Criminal trail, combining technical sections with excellent flow, before continuing toward Thini Village. Transfer to the start of Rebel’s Hideout, followed by a horse ride to Paradise and an equally exhilarating downhill before settling in at Jomsom.

Enjoy a relaxed and scenic cooldown ride to Beni before driving back to Pokhara. The afternoon is free for rest and leisure, followed by an evening celebration in the lakeside city.

Fly back to Kathmandu and spend the day packing bikes and preparing for departure, followed by a farewell dinner to celebrate the journey and shared experiences.

After breakfast, transfer to the airport for your onward flight, marking the conclusion of the Nepal mountain biking adventure.
