EVEREST EXPRESS

EVEREST EXPRESS is a fast-paced MTB adventure through the heart of the Khumbu region. Ride legendary Himalayan trails beneath the world’s highest peaks. Experience Sherpa culture, alpine landscapes, and thrilling descents. A true high-altitude journey built for riders who crave speed and altitude.

COMPLETE TRIP ITINERARY

Upon arrival at Tribhuvan International Airport in Kathmandu, a representative from GNARLY PVT. LTD. will be present to receive and welcome you. You will then be assisted with your transfer to the hotel via private vehicle. Upon arrival at the hotel, a detailed briefing regarding your upcoming tour will be provided.

After breakfast, we drive to Nagarkot and ride the Asia Enduro trails, enjoying a solid 4-5-hour session + a big DH segment to finish off the day. We then drive to Boudhanath, a UNESCO World Heritage site for lunch. Check into our hotel in Thamel by evening.

We begin our journey with a 1-hour Heli flight to Phaplu. Today, we take it easy and settle at our local lodge and Acclimatize with a few warm up rides around the area.

With more than 16 fully built lines in Ratnange—featuring single tracks, technical segments, and everything needed for a great MTB ride—we tackle as many shuttle runs as possible and wrap up the day with a well-earned beer.

There are many different lines to ride in Phaplu. The trail centre has more than 16 different lines altogether. We pick and ride the most beautiful trails today. Afternoon, we chill and enjoy the evening.

We ride more of Ratnange’s trails in the morning, then after lunch pedal toward Junbesi—one of Solu’s must-visit destinations—where we also visit the special Thupten Choling Monastery before settling into the lodge to experience warm Sherpa hospitality in Junbesi.

After breakfast, we pedal toward Lamjure Pass, tackling a hike-and-bike ascent to the top before descending to Jasmane Bhanjyang for an authentic Sherpa lifestyle experience, and we take a good rest tonight in preparation for the challenge that awaits tomorrow.

Today we hike up to Pikey Peak at 4,050 m for a mind-blowing sunrise and views of some of the world’s highest mountains, then—after a coffee—we begin our descent to Dhap on trails featuring everything from single tracks and technical segments to rocks, roots, and open double tracks, stopping for lunch in Jhapre before riding onward to Dhap.

As we prepare to head back to Kathmandu, we enjoy one final trail from Dhap. We will MOTORBIKE or Drive back to Kathmandu in the morning which will be an 8 hour journey. A Farewell dinner will be organized in the evening.

International departure. After breakfast, transfer to Tribhuvan International Airport for your flight home, concluding an unforgettable high-altitude Himalayan mountain biking tour.
