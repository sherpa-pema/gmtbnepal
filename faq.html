We specialise in customised mountain biking tours, with a strong focus on Enduro-style and gravity-oriented downhill riding. Most of our routes feature single-track trails with a combination of technical descents, natural terrain, and a few challenging sections. Our routes have been carefully developed through many years of research, exploration, and reconnaissance across the Himalayas.

Shuttle support is available on most of the rides we organise and operate. A 4x4 support vehicle accompanies the group throughout the trip, providing transportation where road access is available. However, some remote locations are inaccessible by vehicle, adding an element of adventure and physical challenge to the experience. In certain areas, we also utilize horses as an alternative means of shuttle support.

Yes. Our tours are designed to explore different parts of the Himalayan mountains, allowing riders to experience a variety of landscapes, trails, cultures, and destinations. We travel from one location to another with the support of a dedicated vehicle. All bicycles and luggage are transported by vehicle, allowing you to focus on enjoying the ride and the surroundings.

Our tours are primarily designed around Enduro-style riding, with an emphasis on gravity-oriented descents and single-track trails. However, we also accommodate riders interested in cross-country (XC) riding. Upon request, we can design a customised itinerary specifically suited to XC riders and their preferred riding style.

Our tours require a minimum of 4 participants to operate, with a maximum group size of 8 riders. We highly recommend travelling as a private group, particularly with friends or fellow riders you already know. Private groups offer greater flexibility and allow everyone to enjoy the experience together at a comfortable pace.

Kathmandu is situated at approximately 1,400 metres above sea level, while some of our routes reach elevations of 4,200 metres or slightly higher. At these elevations, the air is significantly thinner, making proper acclimatisation essential.

Our itineraries are carefully structured to allow riders to gradually adapt to increasing altitude. The initial stages of the journey are designed to provide sufficient time for acclimatisation before we venture into higher elevations. We also monitor the group throughout the trip and adjust the itinerary when necessary to ensure a safe and enjoyable experience.

Absolutely. We have welcomed many families on our tours, and family members are more than welcome to join the journey even if they are not participating in the rides.

While riders are out exploring the trails, family members can enjoy a variety of activities, including hiking, horse riding, village sightseeing, and visits to monasteries and other cultural attractions. At the end of the day, the group reunites at the same accommodation, allowing everyone to share the experience together.

Yes. Flexibility is one of the key advantages of our customized tours. While we follow a carefully planned itinerary, we also take into consideration the condition, preferences, and energy levels of our guests throughout the journey.

Some riders may prefer additional riding, while others may wish to take a rest day or spend more time exploring their surroundings. We adapt the programme wherever possible to ensure that the experience is comfortable, enjoyable, and suited to the group.

Yes. Selected domestic flights may be included in our itineraries to make the journey more efficient. For example, we may fly between Kathmandu and Pokhara when this provides the most convenient option.

For those looking for an additional adventure, certain domestic flights can be replaced with a motorbike journey, subject to the itinerary and road conditions. We can arrange Honda CRF motorbikes for this option, which has been a popular choice among many of our previous guests. It adds another exciting dimension to the Himalayan experience while allowing you to explore the landscape at a different pace.
