HIMALAYAN MOTO HOLIDAYS “HELLO MOTO”

HELLO MOTO is an unforgettable journey through Nepal, from the vibrant streets of Kathmandu and the beautiful lakes and mountains of Pokhara to the stunning landscapes of the remote Mustang region.

10 NIGHTS 11 DAYS DETAILED SCHEDULE

COMPLETE TRIP ITINERARY

Preparation + Bike test. Replace tires. Check out HONDA CRF, CF MOTO etc. Meet your tour road captains, sweep mechanics, and support crew in Kathmandu for a comprehensive safety briefing, bike ergonomics setup, and equipment inspection before the journey begins.

We begin the morning with a familiarization ride around Pokhara to prepare for the journey ahead. After lunch, we set off on a scenic on-road ride from Pokhara to Besi Sahar, enjoying a relaxed pace and taking in the beautiful landscapes along the way.

Depart BesiSahar in the morning and ascend into the breathtaking Himalayan landscapes of Manang. As the altitude increases, the ride becomes more rewarding and requires extra caution. Explore the tranquil Green Lake, visit centuries-old monasteries, and enjoy Manang's famous apple juice and freshly baked apple pie before descending to Bhratang (2,600m) for the overnight stay.

After breakfast, ride back to the beautiful lakeside city of Pokhara. The afternoon is yours to relax, prepare for the Mustang tour, and experience Pokhara's vibrant cafés, pubs, and lakeside atmosphere.

Ride alongside the legendary Kali Gandaki River as you enter the spectacular Mustang Valley. Continue through dramatic landscapes before visiting the iconic Rebel's Hideout at 3,700 meters. Conclude the day with an overnight stay in Kagbeni.

We start the day by visiting one of the Holiest temples in Mustang (Muktinath). Experience the highlight of the tour with an unforgettable ride to the ancient walled kingdom of Lo Manthang. Discover centuries-old monasteries, immerse yourself in the unique culture of Upper Mustang, and traverse vast high-desert landscapes before returning to Charang for the night.

Follow the historic Kali Gandaki corridor through spectacular mountain valleys and traditional villages. Enjoy a combination of scenic trail riding and cultural exploration before descending to Kalopani for an overnight stay.

Today promises an unforgettable journey through the heart of the Himalayas, following the Kali Gandaki corridor through charming villages, bustling towns, and spectacular mountain scenery. We ride through Baglung, Ridi, Palpa, Butwal, and Syangja before reaching the lakeside city of Pokhara. After an exhilarating day on the road, the evening is reserved for relaxation, with a soothing massage and a peaceful retreat by the lake.

Today we ride from the mountains back to Kathmandu city from Pokhara. Although the journey is extensive, the scenic highways and frequent refreshment stops make for an enjoyable day. Upon arrival, spend the evening exploring the lively streets of Thamel.

Discover Kathmandu's rich cultural heritage with visits to the sacred Pashupatinath Temple, the magnificent Boudhanath Stupa, and the historic Bhaktapur Durbar Square. After lunch, continue to the iconic Swayambhunath (Monkey Temple) for panoramic views of the Kathmandu Valley before gathering for a memorable farewell celebration in the evening.

Transfer to Tribhuvan International Airport for your flight back home, taking with you unforgettable memories of an epic Himalayan motorcycle adventure through the heart of Nepal. Bon Voyage!
