PROGRESSION & COACHING

LEVEL UP WITH
G MTB SKILLS

Ready to ride with total confidence on steep, technical trails? Our PMBIA-certified coaches deliver one-on-one sessions and group clinics covering braking mechanics, body-bike separation, high-speed berm cornering, and steep drop progression.

Level 1: Foundation & Balance

Neutral body position, efficient gear modulation, line selection, and trail safety fundamentals.

Level 2: Flow & Cornering Mastery

High-speed flat turns, berm pumping, bunny hops, wheel lifts, and carrying speed through root sections.

Level 3: High-Altitude Technical Shred

Steep scree switchbacks, rock garden commitment, drop progression, and high-altitude pacing strategies.
