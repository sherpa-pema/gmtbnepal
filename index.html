WHY RIDE WITH US ?

Trails for Every Skill Level

From relaxed family rides to Enduro adventures, our carefully selected trails offer something for every rider, experience level, and riding style.

All-Mountain / Enduro

Experience high-speed flow trails, challenging sections, technical switchbacks, and scenic single tracks winding through pine forests and high-altitude mountain terrain.

Family-Focused

Enjoy safe and enjoyable routes designed to build confidence and keep younger riders engaged, while giving the entire family an opportunity to experience the Himalayas together.

Customised Tours

Every adventure is designed around you. Choose your preferred pace, riding style, duration, rest stops, and overall experience—without being restricted by rigid group schedules.

Fully Supported

Maximize your time on the trails with dedicated 4x4 support throughout the tour. Shuttle to the top, gentle push or pedal to trailhead, and make the most of every descent as you ride through some of the Himalayas’ most spectacular terrain.

MEET THE CREW

Meet the passionate riders, local champions, and logistics masters leading every Himalayan tour.

Founder & Head Guide
TOUR LEADER & ASIA ENDURO HOST

Shyam Gyan Limbu

(Love for MTB and motorbiking since a kid)

Loves to rides trails of Kathmandu, Mustang, Phaplu and Bhutan. Has been in the biking industry for more than almost 2 decades. Organizing MTB racing is one of the most important thing he does. He loves to bring Mtb community together in Asia which is why he has been hosting Asia Enduro Series since 2016. Been guiding since 2012.

Licensed Guide
HIMALAYAN TRAIL & DOWNHILL CHAMPION

Prachit Thapa Magar

(Junior XC & DH Champion • Mustang Trail Specialist)

Licensed guide who has been mountain biking since a very young age. A graduate from Tourism college in Kathmandu, was a junior XC and DH champion in 2018. A guy who doesn’t need a GPS when riding in the trails of Mustang. Has almost a decade of mountain biking and guiding experience in the Himalayas of Nepal.

Logistics & Transport Lead

Tek Shrestha

(4x4 Transport Specialist & High-Altitude Barista)

A perfect guy to manage and organize transportation. He has been driving off-road vehicle for almost 15 years now! He knows every corner of Mustang, Solukhumbu and most parts of Nepal basically! A outdoor coffee barista who can prepare you the best Nepali Coffee at 4000m with a croissant on the side for you to witness the himalayan range.
